---
title: Arrange the workbench
date: 2026-11-06
---

Panels can now be rearranged:

- Drag the dividers to resize panels.
- Hold Option (Alt on Windows) and drag a panel into a split or a tab group.
- Maximize a panel, or use its context menu.

YAFFW remembers your layout, and on narrow windows it switches to a compact layout without losing it.
