---
title: A new editor that runs in the browser tab
date: 2026-06-06
---

YAFFW now opens with a new editor that works on files from your own disk. Open a video, check its details, pick the part you want on the timeline, and export it.

- Unsupported files are rejected when you open them, with the technical details available if you want them.
- The timeline shows the audio waveform. Drag the playhead, click the ruler to seek, and drag the selection edges to trim.
- Export can be cancelled. The result stays in the tab until you download it.
